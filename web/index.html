How it works

Three things, and the third
is the unusual one.

  1. 01

    The vault is a file on your Mac

    Encrypted with a key derived from your master password and a 256-bit account key that is generated once and never transmitted. Lose both and the data is gone — that is the design working, not a bug.

  2. 02

    Sync is optional, and blind

    Devices exchange signed, encrypted changes through a relay. Every change is sealed before it leaves and hash-chained on arrival, so a relay that is compromised — or compelled — can withhold data but cannot read or forge it.

  3. 03

    The extension runs on no page until you ask

    Most managers request access to every site you visit, forever. This one asks the browser for the page only when you invoke it, and the origin it matches against comes from the browser rather than from the page — so a lookalike site cannot claim to be your bank.
